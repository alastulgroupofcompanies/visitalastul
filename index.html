About Alastul

A quantity surveyor’s discipline, built into a group.

Alastul Group is a Johannesburg company established in 2025. It was founded by Angel Masina, a professional with a quantity surveying background, to give project owners, developers and contractors a dependable partner on the numbers, the process and the technology behind a build.

We have commenced with construction project professional services and technology services. Operations are centred on Johannesburg, close to the projects we serve.

Alongside these, the group offers the supply and delivery of construction goods, hospitality, property development and apparel design. Each new line follows the same standard: measured carefully, presented well.

Precision

Costs, quantities and programmes that are measured, documented and open to review.

Grace

Clear communication and considered design, in the way we report as much as in what we build.

Proximity

A Johannesburg team that stays close to the site, the client and the decisions being made.
